MOVENA — MVP Specification Version 1.0

1.  Executive Summary

Movena is a mobility marketplace platform designed to connect e-commerce
SMEs with individuals who already perform regular mobility events (daily
trips such as commuting between cities).

Instead of hiring traditional courier services, SMEs can publish
delivery requests that may be fulfilled by individuals who are already
traveling along compatible routes.

Movena does not generate mobility. It captures existing mobility inertia
and transforms unused cargo capacity into a logistics opportunity.

This model enables: - Lower delivery costs for SMEs - Voluntary extra
income for individuals - A decentralized logistics network

The system functions as a mobility marketplace where logistics
opportunities emerge naturally from daily movement patterns.

2.  Core Philosophy

Movena is built around the following principles:

1.  Mobility is abundant.
2.  People should be free to monetize their natural mobility without
    obligation.
3.  The platform should never pressure users to perform deliveries.
4.  All participation must be voluntary.
5.  Trust between SMEs and couriers must be transparent and observable.
6.  The system should self-regulate through public delivery statistics.

Movena is fundamentally different from traditional delivery platforms
because it does not rely on full-time couriers or delivery quotas.

3.  Target Market (MVP)

Initial geographic scope:

Greater Metropolitan Area (GAM), Costa Rica

This includes cities such as: - San José - Cartago - Heredia - Alajuela

These regions have significant daily commuting flows that Movena can
leverage.

4.  Actors

The MVP includes three actors.

4.1 SME (Business)

An SME is an e-commerce seller who needs to deliver orders.

Responsibilities: - Publish delivery requests - Declare the value of the
order - Offer a delivery reward - Select a courier from interested
candidates

Benefits: - Reduced logistics cost - Access to a decentralized delivery
network

4.2 Courier (Deliverer)

A courier is any individual who wants to monetize their natural
mobility.

Requirements: - Identity verification (DNI) - Profile photo - Acceptance
of platform terms

Couriers must declare mobility events indicating: - Origin -
Destination - Time window - Acceptable deviation distance - Maximum load
capacity (kg)

Couriers are never forced to deliver.

4.3 Movena Platform

Movena orchestrates: - Matching between orders and mobility events -
Escrow payment custody - Courier statistics - Basic risk management -
Guarantee fund operations

Movena acts as a market orchestrator, not as a delivery operator.

5.  Core Concept: Mobility Events

A mobility event represents a real trip that a courier intends to
perform.

Example:

A courier travels daily from Cartago to San José for work.

They may declare a mobility event such as:

Origin: Cartago Destination: San José Departure Window: 6:30–7:30 AM
Maximum deviation: 5 km

The system will only show delivery opportunities that fit within this
mobility constraint.

This ensures that deliveries remain aligned with natural travel
patterns.

6.  Delivery Opportunity Model

SMEs publish delivery opportunities called Orders.

Each order includes: - Pickup location - Delivery location - Declared
order value - Weight of the order - Reward offered to the courier

Couriers whose mobility events are compatible may choose to apply.

The SME can then select one courier.

7.  Commitment Model

The commitment model has two phases.

Phase 1 — Soft Commitment

Occurs when: - Courier applies for a delivery - SME selects the courier

At this stage the courier is expected to present themselves for pickup
within the same day.

Failure to appear increases their cancellation statistics.

Phase 2 — Hard Commitment

Occurs when: - The courier physically picks up the order

At this point: - The courier becomes fully responsible - Movena’s
guarantee fund becomes active - Failure to deliver may result in
permanent ban

8.  Cancellation Rules

Both parties may cancel, but the consequences differ.

Courier cancellation: A courier may cancel freely. However: - The
cancellation is recorded - The courier’s cancellation statistics
increase - SMEs can see these statistics before selecting couriers

No penalties beyond reputation impact.

SME cancellation: SMEs may cancel an assignment before pickup.

If the courier fails to appear: The SME can mark the order as available
again.

SMEs do not receive penalties in the MVP.

9.  Transparency Model

Movena relies heavily on transparent statistics.

Each courier profile exposes: - Total deliveries completed - Total
cancellations - Delivery success rate

This transparency allows SMEs to decide whether to trust a courier.

The system relies on market self-regulation rather than strict
enforcement.

10. Order Constraints (Risk Control)

Maximum declared order value: 100 USD

Orders above this value are not allowed.

11. Guarantee Fund

Movena operates a guarantee fund.

Initial funding source: founder private capital.

Purpose: Compensate SMEs if a courier fails to deliver an order after
pickup.

Coverage: Up to the declared order value (maximum $100).

Long-term goal: self-sustaining guarantee fund.

12. Unit Economic Model

Movena uses a simple commission structure.

SMEs publish a delivery reward (B) for the courier.

Movena takes a 15% commission from the reward.

Example:

Courier reward: $10 SME pays: $11.50

Distribution: Courier: $10 Movena: $1.50

Part of Movena’s commission contributes to operating costs and the
guarantee fund.

13. Unit Economic Event (UEB)

The Unit Economic Event of Movena is defined as:

One successfully delivered order through the platform.

A UEB occurs when:

1.  SME publishes order

2.  Courier is selected

3.  Courier picks up order

4.  Order is delivered

5.  Funds are released

14. Delivery Flow

Typical lifecycle:

1.  SME publishes order

2.  Order becomes visible to compatible couriers

3.  Couriers apply

4.  SME selects courier

5.  Courier arrives for pickup

6.  Order is picked up

7.  Order is delivered

8.  Delivery confirmed

9.  Payment released

15. Identity Verification

Couriers must provide: - National ID (DNI) - Profile photo - Personal
information

Users must accept platform terms.

Severe misconduct may result in permanent ban.

16. Initial Platform Strategy

Launch strategy:

1.  Recruit couriers through social media.
2.  Recruit SMEs operating e-commerce businesses.

Goal: seed both supply and demand sides of the marketplace.

17. MVP Scope Limitations

The MVP excludes:

-   Multi-package deliveries
-   Professional logistics operations
-   Optimized routing algorithms
-   Large item deliveries
-   Deliveries above $100 value

Focus: validate the mobility marketplace model.

18. Success Criteria for MVP

The MVP is successful if it demonstrates:

-   Reliable courier participation
-   SME adoption
-   Successful deliveries
-   Low incident rates
-   Positive marketplace liquidity

These results validate the mobility marketplace concept.
